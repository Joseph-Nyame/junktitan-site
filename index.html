Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does rubbish removal cost?

We charge by volume — how much space your items take up in the truck. There's a minimum charge for very small loads, then pricing scales from 1/8 of a truck up to a full load. Single items have their own price by size. Send a photo on WhatsApp for an exact quote.

Do you offer same-day collection?

Often, yes — depending on our schedule and your location. Call 07825 646222 and we'll tell you the earliest available slot.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Junk Titanic is SEPA-registered, fully licensed and insured. We provide waste transfer documentation for your records.

What happens to my items?

We sort everything. Reusable items are donated to charity where possible, recyclable materials go to authorised facilities, and residual waste is disposed of responsibly. We aim to divert as much as possible from landfill.

Do you cover my area?

We cover Glasgow, Edinburgh, Stirling, Livingston, Falkirk, Kilmarnock, Dunfermline, Dumbarton, Hamilton, Motherwell and the wider Central Belt — plus Loch Lomond by arrangement. If you're unsure, call us.

Will I need to move anything?

No. We collect from wherever items are — inside the house, garage, loft, shed or garden. You point, we do the lifting and loading.
