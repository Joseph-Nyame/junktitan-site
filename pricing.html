Single item removal price

One item? Priced by size.

When you have only one item to remove, Junk Titanic prices are based on the size. For example, a single wardrobe would cost more than a microwave. If you have more than one item, your price switches to truck volume.

Pricing questions

No small print, just answers.

How is my price calculated?

For multiple items, we charge by volume — how much space your items take up in the truck. For a single item, the price is based on the size of the piece. We confirm the exact price once we see the items in person or in a photo.

Do you charge by weight or volume?

By volume, because that's what determines how much truck space your items use. Our trucks hold roughly two tonnes, which is about twice 8 cubic yards (two standard domestic skips) or four full-sized three-seater sofas.

What is the smallest load you take?

We have a minimum charge for very small loads. Send a photo of what you have and we'll confirm whether it falls under the minimum or a 1/8 truck load.

Can I get an exact price before booking?

Yes. WhatsApp a photo and we'll reply with an all-inclusive price — often within minutes. Prefer a site visit? We can come out and quote on the spot.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The price we confirm is the price for the agreed load. If the items are different from the photo, we'll re-confirm the price with you before starting work.

Do you charge extra for my area?

Our listed areas across the Central Belt are covered at standard pricing. If you're further out, send us your postcode with a photo and we'll confirm any travel before you book.
