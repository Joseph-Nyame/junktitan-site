Pricing
All-inclusive pricing
We charge by volume, which is how much space your items take up in the truck. We have a minimum charge for very small loads; from there, prices go from 1/8th of a truck up to a full truckload. We also have special pricing if you only have one single item. We can give you an exact price once we see the items in person.

Single Item Removal Price
When you have only one item to remove, Junk Titanic Prices are based on the size. (For example, a single wardrobe would cost more than a microwave.) If you have more than 1 item, your price is based on truck volume.

Examples of pieces with single-item prices:
Small appliances (microwave, toaster oven, etc)
Computer/computer monitor
Any size mattress (single, double or king-size)
Any appliance (washing machine, dryer, BBQ grill, etc.)
Any furniture (sofa, bed frame, dining table, sofa bed etc.)
Any type of TV
Exercise Equipment (treadmill, elliptical trainers, trampoline etc.)
Single Item Removal
For multiple items? We price by volume.
It’s based on how much space your junk takes up in the truck. We have a minimum charge for very small loads; from there, prices go from 1/8th of a truck up to a full truckload. We can give an exact price when we see the items on site.

Our trucks hold roughly two tons of junk, which is the same as:
Twice 8 cubic yards — equivalent to 2 standard domestic skips
Or, four full-sized three-seater sofas
