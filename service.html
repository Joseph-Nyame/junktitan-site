Full-service uplift

What our rubbish removal service covers

Household, garden, office and construction waste — cleared in one visit.

Whether you're clearing out a few bulky items, emptying a garage, or dealing with the aftermath of a renovation, Junk Titanic provides a full-service rubbish uplift across Glasgow, Edinburgh, Stirling, Livingston, Falkirk, Kilmarnock and the surrounding towns in Scotland's Central Belt.

Our uniformed crew does all the lifting and loading — from wherever items are located — then sweeps up before leaving. You don't need a skip, a van, or a trip to the tip.

Typical items we uplift

  • Sofas, settees, armchairs and sofa beds
  • Wardrobes, drawers, beds and mattresses
  • Washing machines, fridges, cookers and dishwashers
  • TVs, monitors, printers and computer equipment
  • Garden waste, fence panels, sheds and decking
  • Kitchen and bathroom rip-outs
  • General household clutter and bric-a-brac
  • Office furniture, filing cabinets and confidential paper (secure shredding available)

Who it's for

  • Homeowners and tenants clearing junk
  • Landlords and letting agents turning a property around
  • Estate agents and property managers preparing a sale
  • Builders and tradespeople needing a skip alternative
  • Offices, shops and hospitality businesses

Responsible disposal

As a SEPA-registered waste carrier, we sort every load. Reusable items are donated to charity where possible, recyclable materials go to authorised facilities, and residual waste is disposed of legally and responsibly. We provide waste transfer documentation for commercial clients on request.

Good to know

Rubbish removal FAQs

How much does rubbish removal cost?

We charge by volume — how much space your items take up in the truck. There's a minimum charge for very small loads. Send a photo for an exact, all-inclusive quote before you book. See our pricing page for volume examples.

Do you offer same-day collection?

Often, yes — depending on schedule and location. Call 07825 646222 and we'll tell you the earliest available slot.

Do you take everything?

Almost everything non-hazardous. We cannot accept chemicals, asbestos, oil drums, storage tanks or other hazardous waste. If in doubt, call us — our team will advise on safe disposal routes.

Do you cover my area?

We cover Glasgow, Edinburgh, Stirling, Livingston, Falkirk, Kilmarnock, Dunfermline, Dumbarton, Hamilton, Motherwell and the wider Central Belt. See our areas page or call us to confirm.

What happens to my items?

We sort every load. Reusable items are donated to charity where possible, recyclable materials go to authorised facilities, and residual waste is disposed of responsibly. We aim to divert as much as possible from landfill.
